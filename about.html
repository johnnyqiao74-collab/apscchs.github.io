About Our Club

The Asian Peer Support Club (APSC) is a student-led organization at Clarksburg High School is dedicated to fostering a supportive community for Asian students. Our mission is to provide a safe space for sharing experiences, cultural exchange, and personal growth. We welcome all students regardless of their background to join our club and be part of our family to build a stronger and more inclusive community.

We organize regular meetings, workshops, and social events to promote understanding and collaboration among members.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About APSC

Learn more about the Asian Peer Support Club, our programs, and how students can get involved.

What is the Asian Peer Support Club?

The Asian Peer Support Club (APSC) is a student-led organization at Clarksburg High School dedicated to supporting Asian students and creating a welcoming, inclusive community for students from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds. We focus on peer support, cultural connection, academic resources, and community engagement.

What does APSC stand for?

APSC stands for Asian Peer Support Club.

Who can join the Asian Peer Support Club?

APSC is open to all students at Clarksburg High School. You do not need to be Asian to join. Students who are interested in Asian cultures, supporting their peers, or contributing to an inclusive school community are welcome.

What is the purpose of APSC?

Our goal is to build a supportive student community where students can connect with their peers, share experiences, access resources, and feel more comfortable navigating school and cultural differences.

What does APSC do?

APSC organizes student activities, peer-support programs, cultural and educational events, and community initiatives. Our activities may include language programs, peer mentoring, academic support, cultural events, and opportunities for students to connect with one another.

Does APSC only support Asian students?

No. Although APSC focuses on supporting Asian students and addressing experiences that may be particularly relevant to Asian and immigrant students, our programs and community are open to students of all backgrounds.

Does APSC support English language learners?

Yes. APSC has initiatives designed to support students who are learning English or adjusting to an English-speaking school environment. One example is our English Conversation Circle (ECC), which provides opportunities for students to practice conversational English and connect with peers.

What is the English Conversation Circle (ECC)?

The English Conversation Circle is a peer-support initiative that provides students with opportunities to practice English through conversations with other students. The program is designed to help English learners build confidence in communication while creating connections with their peers.

Does APSC provide academic support?

APSC may provide or connect students with peer-based academic resources and support. Our goal is to help students find useful resources and learn from one another.

Does APSC organize cultural events?

Yes. Cultural activities are one way APSC helps students learn about and celebrate Asian cultures while encouraging cultural exchange and understanding within the broader school community.

Is APSC a student-led organization?

Yes. APSC is primarily student-led. Students participate in planning, organizing, and carrying out club initiatives while following the policies and guidelines of Clarksburg High School.

How can I join APSC?

Students interested in joining can follow our club announcements or contact the APSC leadership team for information about meetings, membership, and upcoming activities.

Do I need to be Asian to join APSC?

No. APSC welcomes students of all cultural and ethnic backgrounds. What matters most is your interest in supporting peers and contributing positively to the school community.

Can I volunteer with APSC?

Yes. Students can participate by helping with events, peer-support programs, educational initiatives, outreach, and other club projects.

Where is APSC located?

APSC is based at Clarksburg High School in Montgomery County, Maryland, and primarily serves students within the school community.

How can I contact APSC?

You can contact the APSC leadership team through the contact information provided on our website. We welcome questions, collaboration opportunities, and student participation.

Can other schools or organizations collaborate with APSC?

Yes. APSC is open to appropriate collaboration opportunities with student organizations, schools, community organizations, and other groups whose goals align with our mission.

What makes APSC different from a typical cultural club?

APSC focuses not only on cultural awareness but also on peer support and practical student resources. Our goal is to create programs that help students connect with others, navigate challenges, and participate more confidently in their school community.

Meet the Team

Johnny Qiao

President
+
Johnny is the Founder and currently serving his second consecutive term as President of the Asian Peer Support Club. A dedicated high school leader with a proven track record in organizational management—having previously directed a performance troupe—Johnny established this club with a profound vision: to bridge the gap between cultural transition and personal success.

Under Johnny’s leadership, the organization serves as a cornerstone for students navigating the complexities of a new country and school environment. His mission is to cultivate a premier, inclusive sanctuary where students aren't just adjusting, but are empowered to learn, love, and grow. Johnny is steadfast in his commitment to radical inclusivity, ensuring that the diverse voices of the community are not only heard but are instrumental in shaping the club’s trajectory.

To institutionalize this vision, Johnny leads through the RISE framework:

Resilience: Fortifying the ability of peers to navigate and overcome systemic and personal challenges.

Integration: Facilitating seamless cultural and academic transitions for new members.

Support: Providing a robust infrastructure of peer-to-peer emotional and social resources.

Equity: Championing a platform where every individual has equal access to opportunity and influence.

Johnny’s ambition is to scale this community’s impact, fostering a legacy of excellence and belonging that will endure for years to come. He invites all who share this vision to join the movement in elevating our community to unprecedented heights.
