We Are Here For You

Whether it's academic stress, social challenges, or you just need someone to talk to, APSC is here to listen.

Need immediate help? If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. For emotional distress or suicidal thoughts, call or text 988. APSC provides peer support, not professional or emergency care.
